# Scrawl (experimental)

Scrawl saves the display under your pointer with microphone narration for local playback. Enable **Settings → General → Experimental → Scrawl**. Grant Screen Recording, Microphone, Accessibility and Input Monitoring in macOS System Settings; a blocked pill names the missing permission and opens its setup action.

Press **Option–Shift–S (⌥⇧S)** and release every key to start. The selected display stays fixed for that capture. Speak while demonstrating the issue, then press the shortcut again or click **Finish** to save. Change the shortcut in **Settings → Shortcuts** using a modifier-plus-letter chord. Clicking **✕** asks **Discard?**; click it again to discard. Closing a window or pressing Escape leaves an established capture running.

Scrawl stops after ten minutes, when the recorded display disconnects or changes geometry, or when the Mac sleeps or locks. Starting a meeting finishes the capture and releases its microphone before meeting audio starts. If device teardown takes longer than eight seconds, the meeting reports that it is still pending; retry after teardown finishes. Finish stops screen collection immediately while already captured narration drains. Dictation, Quill and computer-use commands cannot start while Scrawl owns the microphone; an active meeting or other audio activity also prevents a new capture.

Click **Open** on the Ready pill, choose **Open Scrawl Captures…** from the menu bar, or use **Open Scrawl captures…** in General settings. Select a capture to play it, reveal its bundle with **Show in Finder**, or choose **Delete…** and confirm to remove it and all its files. Saved captures remain accessible after disabling Scrawl.

Recording excludes Muesli's application windows, including its indicator, and captures microphone narration without system audio. Files stay on this Mac and appear under **Scrawl captures** in Local storage. Narration is transcribed on device using an installed local model, with selectable timestamped text beneath playback. Audio is transcribed once in disjoint chunks of at most eight seconds, cut at quiet points. Complete measured word times are retained; chunks without complete word times use estimated timing. Parakeet Unified uses estimated timing. Missing models or failed transcription leave the media playable with a named reason. Clipboard summaries are not available yet.

After an interrupted recording, relaunch Muesli to recover playable media. Recovery retains completed video fragments and available narration, resumes missing transcription through the normal finalizer, and reuses any checkpointed transcript. A corrupt or unsupported-version bundle reports its own failure while healthy captures continue recovery. A partial capture may contain only audio or video. The Scrawl window refreshes when processing finishes and shows its recovery state and any failures. A damaged narration file does not prevent surviving video from appearing. If saving fails, Open still selects that capture using its recovery journal. Temporary microphone files use separate app-identity directories and are cleaned on normal stop or the next launch of that identity. Foreground dictation or meetings defer transcription without consuming its active processing timeout.
