## What's new in muesli 0.8.6

- **Bring your Granola conversations with you.** Import notes, summaries and transcripts from Granola ZIP or CSV exports, with a preview before importing.
- **Show it with Vidshots.** Record a window or screen region with spoken narration, then revisit the recording and transcript. Enable Vidshot in Settings → Experimental; captures stay on this Mac.
- **Chat with your meetings and documents.** Attach images, PDFs, Markdown and text files. Document tools let supported models search and read attachments on demand. Original files stay in place; attachment content is sent to the selected AI provider when used.
- **Revisit your day with Time Turner.** Browse and search captured screen history, with recaps and to-dos. Capture is off by default and screen history stays on this Mac. Generating a recap sends sampled text to your selected Meetings provider.
- **Meet Quill and Cotypist.** Ask Quill questions or use spoken instructions to rewrite selected text. Cotypist offers on-device suggestions as you type; it is included but off until enabled. Accepted suggestions are saved in your local Timeline with preceding text and app name.
- **Dictation that fits your workflow.** Watch text appear during hands-free dictation with supported local models, set capitalization and punctuation per app, and dictate while a meeting is recording.
- **Find more across your history.** Search across saved activity and browse the people in your meetings. Calendar-linked recording and call detection are more reliable.
- **More model choices.** Explore new speech-recognition and cleanup models in the model library, plus native tool calling for supported local chat models and endpoints.

### Improvements

- Clearer permission guidance, restart notices and return-to-setup behavior, with protection for active work and unsent chat drafts.
- API credentials stored in Keychain; summary error logs omit provider-supplied details while useful errors remain visible in the app.
- Refined typography, clearer controls, updated What's New cards and small New badges that dismiss after interaction.
- Improved microphone handling, meeting titles, recording controls and shutdown behavior.
